01. About me

Building systems that stay fast when traffic doesn't stay calm.

I'm a Senior Software Engineer based in Ho Chi Minh City; most recently I also served as Solutions Architect at NEYU. Over the past eight years I've designed backend platforms in Golang and Java for telesales, workforce management, logistics, fintech trading and enterprise ERP.

My focus is the hard parts of distributed systems: messaging, caching, database optimization, observability and reliability. I've also been the technical lead for backend teams — setting architecture standards, running code reviews and sprint planning.
